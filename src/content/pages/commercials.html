Commercial Production FAQs

  • Concept and strategy, scripting and storyboards, casting and locations, the shoot, and full post, edit, color, sound, and versioning, all handled by one team.

    Pre-production locks the idea and the logistics, production is the shoot with a professional crew, and post finishes the spot to broadcast standard.

    The output is a polished commercial cut for the placements you are running. Doing all three stages in-house keeps the idea intact from script to final delivery.

  • Yes, and we cut them from the same shoot so the campaign stays consistent across channels. We handle broadcast-standard TV commercials, digital and social video ads, and pre-roll.
    A TV spot, a vertical social ad, and a 6-second bumper are very different edits, but they can share one production, usually the most cost-effective way to cover broadcast, YouTube, Meta, and connected TV at once. Each version is delivered to its platform spec.

  • Broadcast and TV commercials, digital and social video ad production, pre-roll and short bumpers, brand and product spots, and testimonial video production.

    The type follows where the ad runs, a 30-second broadcast spot, a vertical social ad, and a 6-second bumper are built differently even from one idea.

    We usually cut several types from one shoot so the campaign covers TV, YouTube, Meta, and connected TV. On the call we map the types to your placements and budget.

  • About four weeks from concept to final cut, depending on scope, casting, and the number of placements. Pre-production, scripting, casting, and locations, is often the longest phase, followed by the shoot and then post.
