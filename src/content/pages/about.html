Kenyon "Jigsaw" Pruitt

Founder, Owner
Director, Actor, Editor

Kenyon "Jigsaw" Pruitt (born August 4, 1971) is an American film director, producer, and actor. Kenyon first came to prominence as an actor, guest-starring in several music videos, He gained attention for Directing a local film about a corrupt police officer During this time, he also filmed commercials for local Judges and lawyers. He was credited as Kardell Kenyon Pruitt in his film/music video appearances. In 2015, Pruitt left music to focus on directing, producing, and occasionally writing a variety of films and television shows. His films included Hot Pack (2016), Ten Zero Zero (2015), and King of the Streets (2018) as well as Detroit 80's (2019), the police drama Surveillance (2020), Karma (2018), A Little Girls Cry (2018), The Last Drink (2019) The romantic drama short Yasmine Hearns (2021) as well as the award-winning series The Plant (2021)
